Kidcap

Fintech Savings Experience for Kids and Parents

+18% activation 4.8 NPS on onboarding Compliance-ready UX

Kidcap is a savings experience designed for families, pairing mentor-led SIP onboarding with child-friendly learning paths. The product needed to be engaging for kids, transparent for parents, and compliant for finance teams.

Overview

Kidcap makes long-term saving easy for families by blending playful education with transparent finance tools. The goal was to create an onboarding flow that built trust with parents while keeping children engaged through simple interactions and friendly language.

Problem

  • Parents were unsure how to explain SIPs and savings to children.
  • Existing fintech apps felt too complex or too adult for younger audiences.
  • Compliance requirements made onboarding slow and trust-dependent.
  • Families wanted a sense of progress and learning, not just account setup.

Solution

  • Designed a dual-path onboarding experience for parents and kids.
  • Created mentor-led guidance for SIP setup, explaining finance concepts in plain language.
  • Used goal-based interaction and progress trackers to make savings feel rewarding.
  • Built a compliance-ready information architecture for KYC, consent, and documentation.

Process

Research

  • Interviewed parents and financial coaches.
  • Mapped children's attention patterns during digital learning.
  • Analyzed competitor onboarding flows for family fintech.

Design

  • Built low-fidelity flows to align on parent/child dual journeys.
  • Created interactive prototypes with playful visual cues.
  • Validating content tone through usability testing with families.

Impact

  • Improved activation by 18% for first-time family users.
  • Raised first-session satisfaction to 4.8 NPS.
  • Reduced onboarding drop-off by 27% with guided mentor flows.
Kidcap helped families feel confident about long-term savings by making every step clear, visual, and engaging.

Tools Used

  • Figma, Miro, UserTesting, Zeplin, Notion

My Role

  • Led product strategy, UX design, and family-focused research.
  • Designed onboarding journeys for both parents and children.
  • Aligned compliance, product, and marketing teams on the final experience.
