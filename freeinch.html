Freeinch

Award-winning AR Architecture UX for Built Environment Teams

+12% client conversion Streamlined review flow Emerging Product Experience Award

Freeinch is a spatial design tool for architects, engineers, and builders. The product needed to translate complex AR workflows into fast, collaborative review sessions that help teams visualize built spaces and make decisions with confidence.

Overview

Freeinch is an AR-first architecture tool built to help design teams review spaces, annotate models, and align stakeholders using immersive spatial context. The challenge was to make augmented reality feel less like a novelty and more like a practical collaboration platform.

Problem

  • Architecture reviews were fragmented across slides, PDFs, and in-person walkthroughs.
  • Clients struggled to understand 2D plans and spatial relationships without real-world context.
  • AR tools often added complexity instead of clarity for mixed-experience teams.
  • Decision-making slowed when stakeholders could not see the same thing at the same time.
Design review was still stuck in documents. Freeinch had to bring the building into the room without overwhelming users.

Solution

  • Designed a spatial collaboration experience that allows teams to place AR models in context and review them together.
  • Created fast annotation workflows so architects could mark issues and share feedback in real time.
  • Built task-based review modes for clients, builders, and designers to avoid overloading each user.
  • Added offline-friendly AR previews and guided orientation to reduce friction for first-time users.

Approach

The design process centered on spatial understanding and collaboration. I treated AR as a medium for storytelling rather than an interface gimmick.

Stakeholder Research

  • Interviewed architects, contractors, and client representatives.
  • Observed design reviews, model walkthroughs, and on-site coordination.
  • Identified where handoffs broke down and where context was lost.

Prototype & Iterate

  • Built AR concept flows for real-world review sessions.
  • Tested with mixed skill-level teams to keep the experience accessible.
  • Iterated on orientation, annotation, and review modes based on feedback.

Award-Winning Experience

Freeinch received the Emerging Product Experience Award for making AR review approachable, collaborative, and actionable. The judges highlighted the product’s ability to make spatial communication simple for both technical and non-technical stakeholders.

AR review interface designed to simplify spatial collaboration and align mixed-experience teams.

Design Highlights

1. Spatial Clarity for Everyone

Designed an AR orientation sequence that helps users understand scale, placement, and motion before they start reviewing.

2. Collaborative Review Modes

Created separate modes for design critique, client feedback, and site handoff so users see only what they need.

3. Decision-Focused Annotations

Built review cards, issue markers, and actionable notes so feedback translates into work, not vague comments.

Spatial annotation workflows that turn AR review into clear, actionable decisions.
